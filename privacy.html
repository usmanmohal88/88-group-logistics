Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 88 Group Logistics LLC (“88 Group,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information you provide when you use this website. By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described below.

Information you provide

When you submit a shipper quote request, carrier onboarding form, or contact inquiry, we collect the information entered in that form. This may include names, company details, email addresses, phone numbers, shipment locations and schedules, equipment details, carrier identifiers, and any message you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate transportation requests, communicate about potential business relationships, coordinate logistics services, and maintain the security and operation of this website. We do not sell personal information submitted through this website.

Website hosting and service providers

This website may be hosted by Netlify. Form submissions and technical website data may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website and its forms. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process limited technical data, such as server logs, to operate and protect the site.

Retention and security

We retain submitted information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, conduct business, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee that information transmitted over the internet will always be secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit information through a form. If you have a privacy request about information you submitted, use the contact form on our contact page and include enough detail for us to identify your inquiry.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
