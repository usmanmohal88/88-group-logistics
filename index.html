09 Good to know

Frequently
asked.

Have a question not covered here? Send us a note and we’ll be glad to help.

Contact our team ↗
What services does 88 Group Logistics provide?

We provide freight brokerage, truck dispatch and nationwide freight coordination for shippers, carriers and owner-operators.

What equipment types do you work with?

We coordinate Dry Van, Refrigerated / Reefer, Flatbed and Step Deck freight. Let us know your specific requirements when you request a quote.

Do you work with owner-operators?

Yes. Owner-operators and trucking carriers can share their operation details using the carrier onboarding form on this page.

Where do you operate?

We coordinate freight transportation throughout the United States. Share your pickup and delivery locations and we’ll discuss your lane.

How do I request a freight quote?

Complete the shipper quote form with your shipment details. Our team will follow up to discuss the request.

How do I get started as a carrier?

Submit the carrier onboarding form with your company, authority, equipment and contact details. We’ll be in touch about next steps.
